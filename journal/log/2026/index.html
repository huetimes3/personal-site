Tending to my Garden

24.11.2026

Some friends and I were talking over drinks about how we cope with, well, you know. There's a lot going on in the world and things are moving faster than we can keep up. It's easy to slip into a spiral and lose sight of meaning in the quotidian. The conversation somehow trailed to this concept of "tending to one's garden".

I imagine everyone's got a little garden, filled with all the things that they want to spend their time on. When possible, I go to spend a bit of time in my garden. I nourish the plants that are diligently growing there, waiting for attention and care. Life gets in the way, but the garden is always waiting to be watered.

In my garden I've got all the books that I want to get to and all the empty pages of journaling that are waiting for me to scribble something in. I've got game ideas that are sitting on the backburner. I've got friends and loved ones I should be spending more time with, connections I should maintain. I know I should be doing better, but it doesn't help it's all overwhelming.

But then when I really try to think about what's getting in my way, it's mostly things I don't want to do! Doomscrolling is the biggest culprit (I've been getting better tho!), but I've also picked up this nasty habit of keeping up with the news. I don't know how people do it.

So, I take a break from the noise and focusing on something that feels meaningful to me. But it doesn't mean it's easy. Tending to a garden takes at least a little discipline, and I feel the need to consciously remind myself to do the rounds and water my plants.

I wonder how much I got inspired by this post regarding digital gardening. I've surely heard others talk about gardening in the metaphorical sense before, too. Maybe I'll revisit this concept on a later date. After all, this place is yet another planter in my garden.

Feeling Autumnal

10.11.2026

Fall always does something funny to me. Like a tree preparing for winter, I focus all my energy into one last beautiful hurrah. I yearn to create. I suspect it has something to do with the subconscious anticipation of the months that are to come.

I tried to complete inktober. Life got in the way around halfway through, but I'm pretty happy having gotten that far for a first attempt. I might update this with a drawing or two from the month.

I also carved a pumpkin in good company this fall. Well, technically mine was a squash, but I was super happy with how they turned out. Nothing brings friends together like a communal pumpkin gut pot.

Bouldering and Blue Prince with my partner have otherwise dominated my past few months. The former is teaching me that there's a lot I still don't know about myself (I just needed to find the right outlet for my restlessness), and the latter is reminding me of why I got into making games.

I hope I can make a regular habit out of updating here. This fall I'm also learning that I like to write.

"Un deuxième printemps, où toutes les feuilles sont comme des fleurs."
