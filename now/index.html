Feeling Autumnal

Updated 10.11.2026

Fall always does something funny to me. Like a tree preparing for winter, I focus all my energy into one last beautiful hurrah. I yearn to create. I suspect it has something to do with the subconscious anticipation of the months that are to come.

I tried to complete inktober. Life got in the way around halfway through, but I'm pretty happy having gotten that far for a first attempt. I might update this with a drawing or two from the month.

I also carved a pumpkin in good company this fall. Well, technically mine was a squash, but I was super happy with how they turned out. Nothing brings friends together like a communal pumpkin gut pot.

Bouldering and Blue Prince with my partner have otherwise dominated my past few months. The former is teaching me that there's a lot I still don't know about myself (I just needed to find the right outlet for my restlessness), and the latter is reminding me of why I got into making games.

I hope I can make a regular habit out of updating here. This fall I'm also learning that I like to write.

"Un deuxième printemps, où toutes les feuilles sont comme des fleurs."
