Shove Knight

McGame Jame 2021

We wanted to make an action-puzzle game where the player creates a path behind them that would both lead towards their objective and act as the primary obstacle to achieving said objective. One of the first ideas was to have a firefighter that would leave a trail of fire behind them that would spread out of control.

After a bit of refining we dropped the 'action' bit and focused on a grid based enemy shoving puzzler. The floor falls behind the charcter as they navigate a grid and they need to shove the enemies into the newly create pits, before returning back to where they started.

We didn't know this at the time but we had stumbled into making a Sokoban. In retrospect, I'm almost certain we had both played some Sokoban-esque game before coming together for this project. A lot of the design decisions converged to staples of the genre (the quick restart and undo come to mind).

We intentionally kept the scope for this game as small as possible. My friend already had boilerplate for grid based games and we wanted to stick to a monochrome color palette. After the first 24 hours we had pretty much all we needed to get started on churning out levels.

There's some clever levels in there, but also a couple that feel a bit underbaked. Playing more block-pushers after the matter also opened up my eyes to the possibilities (add a footer with a link to hempuli's work! here!). I'd also probably seriously tighten the interpolation time of everything in the game, it kind of gives everything a "mushy" feel right now, idk.

If I haven't somehow convinced you to try a whole library of better sokoban already, you can check out Shove Knight below!
