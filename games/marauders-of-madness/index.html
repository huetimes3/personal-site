Marauders of Madness

GMTK Jam 2023

"Out in the astral seas, aspiring adventurers and heroes must be wary of potential threats. Monsters, pirates and mercenaries sail the stars looking for their next victim. It takes courage, talent, and strategy to survive these islands, and so few heroes ever step up to the task of taming the galaxies."

"None of that matters to you though. You're a pirate! A terror of the seas. You'll cheat, play dirty, and mind control your way to ruin the day of any heroes that might come your way."

Loosely inspired by Captain N'ghathrod, the idea was to make a tactics game where you can't control your troops, and must instead rely on sabotaging the enemy. You would have a limited amount of mind control powers you could allocate to redirect attacks, move the enemy into unfavorable positions, or stun them for a turn.

After the broad strokes of the game's design came together and simple behavior trees were created for a few troop types, I spent most of my remaining time working on the art while my teammate programmed the core loop. For the visuals, I was really into isometric pixel art at the time and it fit the genre. I also have Adam C Younis and his online classes to thank for teaching me the foundations.

Shout out as well to two friends who made the background music. We told them to make a pirate beat and they went lo-fi with a couple "Yo Ho Ho"'s and a couple "Yarr"'s thrown in. Around 2AM during the second night of this project I was starting to feel the effects of sleep deprivation, and one of them decided to help out by putting on a movie. That movie was Beau is Afraid. Totally wasn't planning on going to sleep later that night...

As is the case with jam games in general, we severly overscoped. All that we have to show for our work is a half-baked level, though I hope we can pick this up again. I'll have to first find the project again.
